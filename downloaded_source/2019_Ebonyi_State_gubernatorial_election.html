The 2019 Ebonyi State gubernatorial election occurred on March 9, 2019. Incumbent PDP Governor Dave Umahi won re-election for a second term, defeating APC Sonni Ogbuoji and several minor party candidates.[1][2][3][4][5]

Dave Umahi emerged PDP gubernatorial candidate unopposed.[6][7] He picked Eric Kelechi Igwe as his running mate.[8][9] Sonni Ogbuoji was the APC candidate with Justin Mbam Ogodo as his running mate.[10][11][12] 37 candidates contested in the election.[13]

Electoral system

The Governor of Ebonyi State is elected using the plurality voting system.

Primary election

PDP primary

The PDP primary election was held on September 30, 2018.[14][15][16][17]

Candidates

  • Party nominee: Dave Umahi: incumbent governor
  • Running mate: Eric Kelechi Igwe: incumbent deputy governor

APC primary

The APC primary election was held on September 30, 2018.[18][19][20] Sonni Ogbuoji won the primary election polling 785 votes against 8 other candidates.[21][22][23] His closest rival was Bernard Odoh, the immediate past secretary to the state government of Ebonyi, who came second with 519 votes. Edward Nkwegu came third with 395 votes.[24][25][26][27]

Results

A total number of 37 candidates registered with the Independent National Electoral Commission to contest in the election.[13]

The total number of registered voters in the state was 1,432,528, while 497,291 voters were accredited. Total number of votes cast was 493,002, while number of valid votes was 482,018. Rejected votes were 10,984.[28][29][30][31]
