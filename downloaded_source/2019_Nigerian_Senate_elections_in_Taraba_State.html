Results

Taraba South

A total of 16 candidates registered with the Independent National Electoral Commission to contest in the election. PDP candidate Emmanuel Bwacha won the election, defeating APC Bauka Ishaya Gamgum and 14 other party candidates.[4][5][6][7][8][9]

2019 Nigerian Senate election in Taraba State

PDP Emmanuel Bwacha 96,352
APC Bauka Ishaya Gamgum 51,921

PDP hold

Taraba North

A total of 19 candidates registered with the Independent National Electoral Commission to contest in the election. PDP candidate Shuaibu Isa Lau won the election, defeating APC Yusuf Ahmed Adamu and 17 other party candidate.[10][11][12]

PDP hold

Taraba Central

A total of 11 candidates registered with the Independent National Electoral Commission to contest in the election.[13][14][15][16][17] APC candidate Yusuf Abubakar Yusuf won the election, defeating PDP Dahiru Bako Gassol and 9 other party candidate.[18][19][20][21][22][23]
